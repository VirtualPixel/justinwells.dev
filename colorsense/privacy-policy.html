ColorSense Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

ColorSense is published by CodeBrew, LLC ("we", "us"), 502 W 7th St Ste 100, Erie, PA 16502, United States. This policy covers the ColorSense apps for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac, Android, Wear OS, Windows and Linux.

  • ColorSense works without an account. Without one, nothing you do in the app is sent to us.
  • The camera and the photos you pick are read on your device. We never receive or store images.
  • If you sign in, we keep your email address, your palettes and your Pro purchase records so they can follow you between devices.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

What stays on your device

Identifying colors, the color vision simulation, picking colors from photos, your recent colors and your settings all work on your device. Palettes you save stay on the device. On Apple devices they also sync through your own iCloud account, which Apple runs under its own privacy policy; we can't see that data.

The camera feed and photos are processed on the device to read colors and are never uploaded. When you save a photo with a simulation applied, it goes to your own photo library.

ColorSense account and sync (optional)

You can sign in with your email address to sync palettes between all your devices, including Android, Windows and Linux, and to use ColorSense Pro bought on one platform on the others. When you sign in, we store:

  • Your email address, to send you sign-in codes and identify your account.
  • Your palettes: their names, their colors, and when they were created, changed or deleted.
  • Your Pro purchase records: which store, which product, the store's transaction or order ID and when the purchase expires. We confirm these with Apple or Google. We never receive your payment details.

We use this only to sign you in, sync your palettes and unlock Pro on your devices.

Gift codes

If you redeem a gift code on Android, Windows or Linux, we keep a record of each attempt: the code it was for, your account and email address, the platform, device model and app version, the country the request came from, a one-way hash of your network address, the time and whether it worked. We use it to see how gift codes are used and to stop people guessing them.

Watches

The Apple Watch app gets your palettes through iCloud. The Wear OS app gets them from ColorSense on your phone through Google's Wearable Data Layer, which stays between your own phone and watch.

Feature requests

In the iPhone and iPad app, you can suggest and vote on features. That board is run by WishKit. It receives the text you write, your votes and an anonymous identifier, plus an email address only if you choose to give one.

Logs

When you are signed in, our hosting provider keeps standard request logs, such as network addresses and times, for a limited period, to run and secure the service.

How long we keep it

Account data stays until you delete your account. Deleted palettes are kept as small deletion markers so your other devices remove them too. Gift code records are kept to show how each code was used. Once your account is deleted, they no longer carry your email address or a link to your account.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings, then Account and Sync, then Delete Account. That deletes your account, the palettes stored with it on our servers and its purchase records. Palettes stay on your device. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription; manage that in the App Store or Google Play.

No longer have the app? Email justin@justinwells.dev from the address you signed in with and we will delete the account within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it, wherever you live, by emailing justin@justinwells.dev. Residents of places such as the European Union, the United Kingdom and California have these rights by law. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising.

Children

ColorSense isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent between the apps and our servers is encrypted in transit. Each account can only read its own data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date above.
